Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Ozzie ("we", "us") provides an AI-powered messaging assistant that businesses use to reply to their customers on WhatsApp and Instagram. This policy explains what data we process and how, for both the businesses that use Ozzie and their customers who message those businesses.

What we collect

  • Messages sent to a business's WhatsApp or Instagram account, and Ozzie's replies
  • Basic conversation metadata (timestamps, sender identifiers such as phone number or Instagram-scoped ID)
  • Business information provided by our clients (hours, prices, services) used to answer customer questions
  • Booking details customers share in conversation (name, requested service, date/time)

How we use it

  • To generate accurate, relevant replies to customer messages
  • To let the business owner review conversations and take over a chat at any time
  • To record bookings made through the conversation

We do not sell customer data, and we do not use conversation content for advertising.

How we store it

Conversation data is stored securely with our database provider (Supabase) and is only accessible to the relevant business and to Ozzie's operating team for support and debugging.

Third parties

To operate, Ozzie sends message content to Anthropic (our AI provider) to generate replies, and uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and Instagram Messaging API to send and receive messages. Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy terms.

Your choices

Customers can ask the business at any time to speak with a human instead of the assistant. Businesses can pause or disable Ozzie for any conversation, or for their account entirely, at any time.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your data, email hello@hiozzie.com with the phone number or Instagram handle used to message the business, and we will remove it within 30 days.
