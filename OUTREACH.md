# NeverMiss — Outreach (cold email)

Regla de oro: cortos, imperfectos, sin diseño, sin logos, como si el dueño de un negocio pequeño le escribiera a otro. Nada de "I hope this email finds you well".

## Cómo sacar la lista (sin hablar con nadie)
1. Google Maps → busca "barber north vancouver", "dental clinic kitsilano", "restaurant gastown", etc.
2. De cada negocio: nombre, web, email (está en su web, sección contact) e Instagram.
3. Apunta algo específico de cada uno (una reseña, su horario, algo de su web) — se usa en la primera línea.
4. Meta: 20 negocios por tanda. Enviar 10 al día máximo (evita spam filters). Gmail normal sirve para empezar.

## Email 1 — el directo

**Asunto:** quick question about {Business}

> Hey — I messaged a few local {barbershops} last week and most never replied. Not judging, I know you're cutting hair, not staring at a phone.
>
> I run a small service here in Vancouver that answers your WhatsApp/Instagram messages for you — prices, hours, bookings — in seconds, any hour. It sounds like you, not like a robot.
>
> You can try it yourself in 30 seconds here (no signup): {link}
>
> If it's not for you, no worries at all.
>
> {Nombre}

## Email 2 — el de la reseña

**Asunto:** saw a review of {Business}

> Hey — I was reading reviews of {Business} (the one about {detalle específico} made me laugh). One thing I see with busy {salons} like yours: people message at 9pm to book and by morning they've booked somewhere else.
>
> I built something that answers those messages for you, instantly, 24/7. Try texting it here like a customer would: {link}
>
> Worth 30 seconds of your time. If not, delete this and have a great week.
>
> {Nombre}

## Email 3 — el ultra corto

**Asunto:** your missed messages

> How many WhatsApp messages did {Business} miss this week?
>
> Mine answers all of them, in seconds, at any hour, and books the appointment. Try it (30 sec, no signup): {link}
>
> {Nombre}

## Follow-up (5 días después, solo uno, responder al mismo hilo)

> Hey — just floating this back up. The demo takes 30 seconds: {link}
> If it's a no, totally fine, I won't write again.

## Notas
- {Nombre}: un nombre normal, consistente en todo (emails, landing, Stripe).
- Firmar sin cargo ni empresa grandilocuente. Como mucho: "{Nombre} · NeverMiss · Vancouver".
- El link es la landing con la demo. La demo vende; el email solo consigue el clic.
- Cambiar {barbershops}/{salons} según el nicho de la tanda.
